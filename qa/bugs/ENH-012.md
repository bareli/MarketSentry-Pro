## Summary

Builds on ENH-011 (quantity held): once a position has a quantity, adding an optional "cost basis"
(buy price) lets the integration show unrealized gain/loss - a natural next step for anyone who sets
up a position at all, and still read-only/display-only, no trading.

## Recommended change

Add an optional "cost basis per unit" field alongside ENH-011's quantity field; when both are set,
expose `unrealized_pnl`/`unrealized_pnl_pct` attributes per instrument. Depends on ENH-011 landing
first (shares its config-flow field and schema shape).

## Implementation (2026-10-31, D-031)

`OPT_COST_BASIS` in `const.py`, same wiring as `OPT_QUANTITY`. `_unrealized_pnl(quantity, cost_basis,
price, is_outcome)` in `sensor.py`, same percent-scaling convention as `_position_value`.
`unrealized_pnl`/`unrealized_pnl_pct` attributes on `ProbabilitySensor`/`PriceSensor`/`RateSensor`,
correctly excluded from the recorder via `_attr_unrecorded_attributes` in all 3 (done right the
first time, unlike ENH-011 which needed this fixed during review). No aggregate P&L sensor added -
out of this record's stated scope, flagged as a possible follow-up rather than built unprompted.
Translations mirror the `quantity` field's pattern in `strings.json` and all 8 locales. Commit
`866757a`.
