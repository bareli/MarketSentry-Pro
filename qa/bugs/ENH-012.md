## Summary

Builds on ENH-011 (quantity held): once a position has a quantity, adding an optional "cost basis"
(buy price) lets the integration show unrealized gain/loss - a natural next step for anyone who sets
up a position at all, and still read-only/display-only, no trading.

## Recommended change

Add an optional "cost basis per unit" field alongside ENH-011's quantity field; when both are set,
expose `unrealized_pnl`/`unrealized_pnl_pct` attributes per instrument. Depends on ENH-011 landing
first (shares its config-flow field and schema shape).
