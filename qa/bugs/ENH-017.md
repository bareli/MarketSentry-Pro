## Summary

UX-004 (closed) already addressed the threshold/hysteresis fields' missing units/help text. This
goes further: even with units labeled, a user still has to guess whether e.g. a 2% move threshold
is tight or loose for a specific instrument - a volatile crypto coin and a stable FX pair need very
different defaults, and the form gives no feedback either way.

## Recommended change

Once the instrument is resolved (confirm step already has `ref` and can call the provider), fetch a
short recent price sample and show it as a one-line hint next to the threshold fields (e.g. "AAPL
moved ~1.2% on an average day over the last week") so the default/entered value can be judged
against something real, not a kind-agnostic guess.
